# NDA Clause Library

> Document ID: VLH-CON-003 | Owner: Corporate & Commercial Team | Version 2.4
> Use these clauses when preparing or reviewing a mutual non-disclosure agreement (NDA).

## 1. Definition of Confidential Information

Preferred wording:

```text
"Confidential Information" means all information, in any form, disclosed by or on behalf of
the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party in connection with the Purpose, that is marked
confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential, including
technical data, pricing, business plans and personal data.
```

## 2. Standard Exclusions

```text
Confidential Information does not include information that (a) is or becomes public other than
through breach of this Agreement, (b) was lawfully known to the Receiving Party before
disclosure, (c) is independently developed without use of Confidential Information, or
(d) is lawfully received from a third party without restriction.
```

## 3. Term of Obligations

```text
The obligations of confidentiality continue for three (3) years from the date of last
disclosure. Obligations in respect of trade secrets continue for as long as the information
remains a trade secret.
```

## 4. Return or Destruction

```text
Within fifteen (15) days of a written request, the Receiving Party shall return or securely
destroy all Confidential Information and confirm destruction in writing, except for copies it is
required to retain by law.
```

## 5. Remedies

```text
The Receiving Party acknowledges that damages may not be an adequate remedy and that the
Disclosing Party may seek injunctive relief without proof of actual loss.
```

## 6. Governing Law

```text
This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. Disputes shall be resolved by arbitration in
Mumbai by a sole arbitrator.
```

## Fallback Positions

| Clause | Standard position | Acceptable fallback | Needs approval from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Term of obligations | 3 years | 5 years | Corporate Counsel |
| Return or destruction | 15 days | 30 days | Associate Counsel |
| Governing law | India | Singapore (foreign counterparty) | Senior Corporate Counsel |
| Non-solicitation of employees | Not included | 12 months, deal-related NDAs only | Corporate Counsel |
