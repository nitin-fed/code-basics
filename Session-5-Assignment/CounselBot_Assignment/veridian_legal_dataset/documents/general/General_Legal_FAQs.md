# Veridian Legal Hub: General Legal FAQs

> Version 4 | Owner: Office of the General Counsel | Applies to all Veridian staff

## Getting Legal Help

### How do I request legal help?
Raise a request on the **LegalDesk** portal. Emails and chat messages are not tracked. Include the business context, the deadline and any draft documents.

### How fast will Legal respond?
A standard NDA review takes 2 business days. A contract below ₹50 lakh takes 5 business days. A request marked urgent is handled faster, but urgency must be justified in the request.

## Contracts and Signatures

### Can I sign a contract myself?
Only authorised signatories can sign on behalf of Veridian, and only within their delegated limits. Never sign a contract that Legal has not reviewed.

### Do I need an NDA before sharing information with a vendor?
Yes. Use the standard mutual NDA from LegalDesk before you share anything confidential. Do not edit the template yourself.

## Confidentiality and Privilege

### What does "privileged" mean?
Legal advice given in confidence to Veridian is protected from disclosure. Marking an email *Privileged & Confidential* helps, but forwarding it widely can destroy the protection.

### Why can I not see some legal matter files?
Sensitive matters are handled by named matter teams behind an **ethical wall**. If you are not on the team, you will not be able to see the matter files, and you must not ask team members for details.

## Records and Holds

### What is a legal hold?
A legal hold is an instruction to keep all records relevant to a dispute or investigation. When you receive one, stop deleting anything related to the matter and acknowledge the notice within 5 business days.

### How long are emails kept?
Email and chat messages are kept for 3 years, unless a legal hold applies.

## Reporting Concerns

### How do I report a suspected violation?
Use the Speak-Up hotline or portal. You can stay anonymous. Retaliation against someone who reports in good faith is not tolerated.

### I received a legal notice at my desk. What should I do?
Do not reply. Send a scan to the Litigation team on LegalDesk on the same day. Notices carry short deadlines.
